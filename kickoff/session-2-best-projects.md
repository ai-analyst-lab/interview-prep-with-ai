# Session 2: Your best projects (about 30 min)

Pick three to five projects you're proud of. For each one, just tell the story. Don't worry about the order.

## Questions (per project)
1. What was the situation? What problem or question kicked it off, and who asked?
2. Why did it matter? What decision, money, or users were on the line?
3. What did you personally do? Walk through the actual work: data, analysis, method, tools.
4. What was hard or surprising? Where did you get stuck, and how did you get unstuck?
5. What did you find, and what did you recommend?
6. What happened because of it? What changed, and by how much?
7. If you did it again, what would you do differently?

## Try to cover a mix
- An analysis that changed a decision
- An experiment or A/B test you designed, ran, or had to interpret (including one that didn't go as planned)
- A metric, dashboard, model, or data source you built that people still use
- Something you did that no one asked you to do
- (PMs) A launch you drove with data, and how you measured it
- (Finance) A forecast or variance you had to explain to leadership

## Listen for
- The result and the number. If you don't know the exact number, say the range.
- The decision it changed. "The team shipped X" or "we stopped Y."
- The method, in one sentence, before the details.
