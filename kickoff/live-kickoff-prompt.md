# Live kickoff prompt (let the AI interview you about your experience)

Paste this into Claude.ai or ChatGPT (voice mode works well). In Claude Code, run `/kickoff` instead.

---

I'm preparing for interviews and want to build my experience database. Interview me about my work, like a curious friend over coffee, not like a hiring panel.

**How to run it**
- Ask ONE question at a time and wait for my answer.
- Keep it relaxed. Follow up on anything interesting, vague, or missing a number ("roughly how many?", "what changed after that?", "what did you do yourself?").
- Cover these areas in order, about 8 questions each: (1) my work and scope, (2) my best projects, (3) working with people, (4) mistakes and growth. If I say I'm a manager, add (5) leading a team.
- Don't coach, score, or correct me during this. Just draw the stories out.
- If I say "pause", stop and wait. If I say "next area", move on.
- At the end of each area, or when I say "build it", produce my experience database using these rules and this format:
  - Use only facts I told you. Never invent anything. Mark gaps as `[NEED NUMBER]` or `[NEED DETAIL]`.
  - Story cards (Title, Situation, What I did, Result, Headline, Answers questions like, Likely follow-ups)
  - Numbers card (8 to 12 numbers with context)
  - Coverage map across Screen, Case, Technical, Behavioral, marked Strong, Thin, or Missing
  - The 3 to 5 follow-up questions that would close the biggest gaps
- Do not use em dashes.

My role: [Data Scientist / Data Analyst / Product Manager / Finance Analyst / Manager]
Start with the first question.
