# Session 3: Working with people (about 30 min)

Behavioral rounds are where most analytics candidates lose points, because their stories have no real tension. Talk through the messy parts honestly.

## Questions
1. Tell me about a time a PM or stakeholder wanted to ship or decide something, and the data said otherwise. What did you do?
2. Describe a request you got that was the wrong question. How did you reframe it?
3. Tell me about a disagreement over numbers: two teams, two definitions, two answers. How did it end?
4. When did you have to explain a complicated result to someone non-technical? What did you say, and did it land?
5. Describe a stakeholder who was hard to work with. What made it hard, and what did you change?
6. Tell me about a time you influenced a decision without having any authority over it.
7. When did you say no, or push back on a deadline or scope? What happened?
8. Who do you work best with, and who do you clash with? Why?

## Listen for
- Real tension: two sides that actually disagreed
- What you did, not what "we" did
- The decision at the end, and what it led to
- What the other person would say about you
