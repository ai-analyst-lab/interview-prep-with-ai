# Session 1: Your work and scope (about 30 min)

Talk casually, like you're explaining your job to a friend who works in tech. Don't polish. Tangents are fine.

## Questions
1. What's your current role, and what do people actually come to you for?
2. Which products, teams, or business areas do you cover? How big are they (users, revenue, or anything else that gives a sense of scale)?
3. Which metrics or numbers are you responsible for, or known for? Who looks at them and how often?
4. Who depends on your work? Name the roles (PMs, engineers, finance, leadership) and what they use it for.
5. Walk through a normal week. What takes most of your time?
6. What tools do you use every day (SQL, Python, spreadsheets, BI tools, experimentation platforms, AI tools)? How hands-on are you?
7. How do you use AI in your work today? Give one specific example.
8. What's the biggest thing that would break or slow down if you left tomorrow?
9. How has your scope grown over the last one to two years?
10. Before this role, what did you do, and what's the thread that connects your jobs?
11. Which products or companies do you genuinely use and care about? Tell me about a moment one of them mattered to you, even a small one. (This becomes your "why this company" answer.)

## Listen for (the AI will ask about these if you skip them)
- Scale: users, revenue, team size, number of stakeholders
- Ownership words: "I own", "I built", "I decided"
- Anything you described as "just" or "only". That's usually underselling.
- Real moments with a product you use. Those make "why this company" answers sound like you, not a mission statement.
