# Extraction prompt (turn your kickoff transcript into an experience database)

Paste this into Claude.ai or ChatGPT, then paste your transcript underneath. In Claude Code, run `/kickoff extract` instead.

---

Below is a transcript of me talking casually about my work experience. It is unstructured on purpose. Turn it into my experience database for interview prep.

**Rules**
- Use only facts from the transcript. Never invent a project, number, title, team size, or result.
- If a story is missing a fact an interviewer would ask for, write `[NEED NUMBER]` or `[NEED DETAIL]` in its place.
- Keep my own words where they are strong. Mark anything you wrote yourself as `(suggested)`.
- Flag any place where I undersold myself ("just", "only", "helped with", "small project") and show what the stronger, still-true version might be.
- Do not use em dashes.

**Output, in this order**

1. **Story cards.** One per project or situation, in this format:
   - **Title:** a short name I can remember
   - **Situation:** one or two lines
   - **What I did:** the actions that were mine
   - **Result:** what changed, with the number (or `[NEED NUMBER]`)
   - **Headline:** one sentence, result first, that I could open an answer with
   - **Answers questions like:** 2 to 4 common interview questions this story fits
   - **Likely follow-ups:** 2 questions an interviewer would push on

2. **Numbers card.** The 8 to 12 numbers I should be able to say without thinking (scale, results, team size, time saved), each with one line of context.

3. **Product moments.** Any real moment I described with a product or company I use, written as a two-sentence opener for "why do you want to work here?". Skip this if there are none.

4. **Coverage map.** A table of common questions for the four rounds (Screen, Case, Technical, Behavioral) with my role in mind, each marked **Strong** (a story with a result and number), **Thin** (a story but missing pieces), or **Missing** (no story yet).

5. **Follow-up questions.** The 3 to 5 questions I should answer next to close the biggest gaps, most important first. Ask them one at a time if I say "go".

My role: [Data Scientist / Data Analyst / Product Manager / Finance Analyst / Manager]
