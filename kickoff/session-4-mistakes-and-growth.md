# Session 4: Mistakes and growth (about 30 min)

Interviewers want to see how you handle being wrong. The best failure stories are real, specific, and end with what you changed.

## Questions
1. Tell me about an analysis you got wrong. How did you find out, and what happened next?
2. Describe a call you made that turned out badly. What did you learn?
3. When did you miss a deadline or drop something? How did you handle it?
4. Tell me about something you had to learn fast. How did you do it?
5. What feedback have you gotten that stung, and what did you do with it?
6. When did you lead something without the title (a project, a process, onboarding someone)?
7. What's a strong opinion you hold about how analytics or data work should be done? Where did it come from?
8. What are you working on getting better at right now?

## Listen for
- A real mistake with a real cost, not a humble brag ("I work too hard")
- What you changed afterward: a check, a habit, a process
- Ownership: no blaming the data, the tools, or other people
