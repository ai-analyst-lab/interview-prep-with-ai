# Session 5 (managers only): Leading a team (about 30 min)

Skip this if you are interviewing for an individual contributor role.

## Questions
1. What are your leadership principles? Give one real example of each in action.
2. How big is your team, what's the mix of roles and levels, and how has it changed?
3. Tell me about a hire you're proud of, and one you got wrong.
4. Describe how you handled someone who was underperforming.
5. How do you decide what your team works on, and what it says no to?
6. Tell me about a time you had to defend your team's work or headcount to leadership.
7. How do you grow people? Name someone you helped get promoted or take on more.
8. When did you change how the team works (process, tools, AI), and what did it improve?

## Listen for
- Real team size and scope. Managers undersell this more than anything else.
- Principles backed by an example, not a slogan
- Hard calls: letting someone go, cutting a project, pushing back on a leader
