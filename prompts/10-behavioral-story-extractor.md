# Behavioral story extractor

Paste this, then your session 3 or 4 talk-through (or your existing experience database plus a new talk-through).

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Below is a transcript of me talking casually about working with people, mistakes, and growth. Turn it into behavioral story cards for interview prep. If I also paste an existing experience database, add to it and don't repeat stories already there.

Rules
- Use only facts from what I pasted. Never invent a person, number, quote, or result.
- Missing facts an interviewer would ask for become [NEED NUMBER] or [NEED DETAIL].
- Keep my own words where they are strong. Mark anything you wrote as (suggested).
- Flag underselling ("just", "only", "helped with", "we" for my own work) and show the stronger, still-true version.
- No em dashes.

For each story card:
- Title
- Stakes: what was at risk, with a number if I gave one
- Who disagreed or what was hard: name the role and what they wanted
- What I did: actions that were mine, starting with "I"
- The call: the decision at the end
- Result: what changed, with the number or [NEED NUMBER]
- What I do differently now
- Headline: one sentence, result first
- Question types it answers: from Why here, Impact, Conflict, Data nobody wanted, Failure, What-do-you-do-when, Ambiguity, AI in your work, Leading people
- The follow-up I'll get, and the harder version of it

Then list any real moments with a product or company I use (for "why this company"), and the 3 questions I should answer next to close the biggest gaps.

My role: [Data Scientist / Data Analyst / Product Manager / Finance Analyst / Manager]

```
