# Cold retest

Taught is not known. A topic counts as learned after 3 correct cold answers on 3 separate days. Paste the queue table the AI gave you last time (or keep it in `debriefs/retest-queue.md`). In Claude Code, run `/retest` instead.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Quiz me on my retest queue, cold.

Rules
- Ask ONE question at a time from my queue, in random order. Do not teach, hint, or explain anything before I answer.
- After my answer, mark it Correct or Not yet, with one line on why. If Not yet, give me the single fix, then move on.
- Never mark a topic done because you explained it. A topic counts as learned only after 3 correct cold answers on 3 separate days.
- You can't see my other sessions, so at the end give me the updated queue as a table I can save and paste back next time: Topic | Correct cold (out of 3) | Last correct date | Status (Learning or Learned).
- No flattery. Do not use em dashes.

Today's date: [date]
My retest queue: [paste the table from last time, or the "Add to retest queue" lists from your debriefs]
```
