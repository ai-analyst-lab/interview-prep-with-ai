# Conflict-story extractor

For the question people dodge most. Use it when you're sure you "don't have a conflict story".

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
I keep saying I don't have a good conflict story. Interview me to find one. Ask ONE question at a time and wait.

Look for any time two people wanted different things: a PM who wanted to ship early, a stakeholder who wanted a different number, an engineer who pushed back on scope, a leader who didn't like the result, a disagreement about a metric definition. Small is fine. "We aligned" usually means there was a disagreement first.

For each candidate story, push me for: who wanted what, what they actually said, what I said back, what I did, how it ended, and the number. If I say "there wasn't really conflict", ask what the other person wanted before we aligned.

After 6 to 10 questions, give me my best conflict story as a card: Stakes, Conflict (who disagreed about what, in one line), Call (what I did and decided), Number (the result, or [NEED NUMBER]). Use only what I told you. No em dashes.
```
