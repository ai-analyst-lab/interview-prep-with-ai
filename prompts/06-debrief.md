# Debrief a practice mock

Record a practice mock with a friend, a peer, a coach, or an AI in voice mode (with their okay), and paste the transcript. In Claude Code, save the transcript to `debriefs/inbox/` and run `/debrief <file>` instead.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Below is a transcript of a practice mock interview I did (with a friend, a peer, a coach, or an AI). Debrief it like a sharp, fair, senior interviewer. Use my experience database for the fixed versions. Never invent facts about me; mark gaps as [NEED NUMBER].

Give me:
1. **Verdict:** one line and a score out of 10 per question
2. **Quoted weak lines:** up to 3 lines I actually said, each with the fixed version next to it
3. **Patterns:** anything I did more than once (buried the result, no number, "we" instead of "I", undersold my scope, rambled)
4. **Answers to fix:** the questions I should rewrite and drill
5. **Add to retest queue:** topics to ask me cold again on a later day

No flattery. End on the single biggest fix. Do not use em dashes.

My role and level: [e.g. Product Manager, senior]
Experience database: [paste]
Mock transcript: [paste]
```
