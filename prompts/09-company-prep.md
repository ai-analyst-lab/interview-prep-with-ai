# Company prep

Research the company's business model, key metrics, and likely case topics from public information only. Don't paste anything confidential.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
I have an interview for [role] at [company]. Help me prep using public information only: the company's website, product, public filings, earnings calls, press, and blog posts. Browse if you can.

Give me:
1. **Business model:** how the company makes money, in 3 to 5 lines. Who pays, for what, and how often.
2. **Key metrics:** the 5 to 8 metrics that likely matter most to this business and to my target team, and why.
3. **Likely case topics:** 5 case or product questions I could plausibly get for this role, based on the business and its launches, pricing changes, or strategy shifts from the last year. For each, the metric I would anchor on.
4. **Questions to ask them:** 5 sharp questions for my interviewers that show I understand the business.

Rules
- Public information only. If you're not sure something is true, say so. Never make up a number or a fact about the company; label assumptions as (assumption).
- If you browsed, list your sources at the end.
- Do not use em dashes.

Then connect it to me: which 2 or 3 stories from my experience database fit this company best, and why.

Experience database: [paste]
```
