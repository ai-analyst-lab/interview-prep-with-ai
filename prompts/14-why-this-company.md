# "Why this company" builder

Use the product for a few days first, and write down what you notice. Then paste this.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Help me build my answer to "Why do you want to work here?" for [company], for a [role] role.

My notes from using the product: [what I used, what I noticed, what I liked, what annoyed me]
Why the company matters to me: [anything real: I use it, I believe in what it's for, a problem I care about]
My experience database: [paste]

Build a 45 to 60 second answer in three parts:
1. My own connection: one real, specific moment with the product or its purpose, from my notes. If I gave you nothing real, ask me for one. Do not make one up.
2. One specific observation from using the product, ideally something I could measure.
3. The story from my experience database that shows what I'd bring to that problem, with a number.

Then:
- Check it with this test: could another candidate say this word for word? If yes, show me which line is generic and ask me for the real detail.
- Give me one follow-up they're likely to ask ("what do you use it for?", "what would you change?") and the short answer from my notes.
- Flag anything that sounds like perks, comp, or "I'm exploring". Those don't belong in this answer.
- No em dashes.
```
