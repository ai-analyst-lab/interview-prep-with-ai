# Fixed answers builder

The seven screening questions every candidate gets asked in some form (from [`../interviewer/coaching-rules.md`](../interviewer/coaching-rules.md)). Write them once, save them in `me/fixed-answers.md`, and practice until they sound natural.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Help me write my fixed answers: the screening questions every candidate gets asked in some form. I'll write these once and practice them until they sound natural, so I never compose them live.

The questions:
1. Walk me through your background.
2. What do you own today? (scope, team, metrics, who depends on you)
3. How hands-on are you? (SQL, Python, tools, how often)
4. How do you use AI in your work?
5. Why are you looking, and why this role?
6. What are your compensation expectations?
7. Where else are you interviewing?

Rules
- Use only facts from my experience database below. Never invent anything. Mark gaps as [NEED NUMBER] or [NEED DETAIL].
- Result first, at least one number where it fits, "I" not "we".
- Keep each answer under 60 seconds spoken. Question 1 can run to 90 seconds.
- For questions 6 and 7, write a short, polite answer that keeps my options open. Leave the specifics as [MY RANGE] and [MY STATUS] for me to fill in.
- Do not use em dashes.

Then quiz me: ask the 7 questions one at a time in random order. After each answer, tell me where I drifted from my written version and the one fix.

Target role and level: [e.g. Senior Data Scientist]
Experience database: [paste]
```
