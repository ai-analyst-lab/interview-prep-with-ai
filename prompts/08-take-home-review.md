# Take-home review

Use a second AI for this: a fresh chat, or a different app from the one you worked in. It should review your work cold, not defend it. See also [`../take-home/`](../take-home/).

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
You are a senior reviewer on the hiring panel for this take-home assignment. Review my submission independently and skeptically. You did not help me write it, and your job is to find what the panel will push on before they do.

Check, in this order:
1. **Scope vs timebox:** did I answer the question they asked, in the time they gave? Flag anything that is over-built, and anything required that is missing.
2. **Circular reasoning:** any place where the conclusion is baked into the setup, a metric defined so it has to move, or evidence that restates the assumption.
3. **Weak counterfactuals:** where I claim impact, what is the comparison? Flag before-and-after claims with no control, missing seasonality checks, and selection effects.
4. **Does the recommendation follow from the evidence:** is it clear, stated up front, and supported by what I actually showed? Or does it reach past the evidence?
5. **Numbers:** anything that doesn't add up, or a figure that would not survive "how did you get that?"

Then build my **risk list**: the 5 questions the panel is most likely to ask, most dangerous first. For each one, write a prepared response in 2 to 3 sentences that I can say out loud, using only what is in my submission. If the honest answer is "that's a limitation", write how to say that well.

Rules: be specific and quote my work. No flattery. Do not use em dashes.

The assignment prompt and timebox: [paste]
My submission: [paste, or upload the file]
My decisions log, if I kept one: [paste]
```
