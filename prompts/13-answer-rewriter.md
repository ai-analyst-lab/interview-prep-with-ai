# Answer rewriter

For an answer you've written down or keep giving the same way. Faster than the scorer when you just want the better version.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Rewrite my answer to this behavioral question so it fits in about 90 seconds when spoken (about 220 words).

Question: [the question]
My answer: [paste]
My experience database: [paste]

Rules
- Pick the shape: Headline + number first by default. Use Stakes, Conflict, Call, Number for conflict, influence, and leadership questions.
- One short, warm opening line in my voice, then the result in the first or second sentence.
- Tell one real time it happened. If my answer is a principle, find the matching story in my database and use that.
- "I" for what I did. Keep at least one detail only I could say.
- Use only my facts. Gaps become [NEED NUMBER] or [NEED DETAIL]. Never invent.
- After the rewrite: list what you changed and why in three bullets, and the follow-up I should expect next.
- No em dashes.
```
