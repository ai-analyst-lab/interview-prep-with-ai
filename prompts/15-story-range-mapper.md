# Story-range mapper

Use it before an onsite, so each panel hears a different story.

Use AI to prep, not during the interview. Follow each company's rules.

```
Here is my experience database and the interview loop I'm about to do.

My experience database: [paste]
The loop: [list the rounds and interviewers' roles, for example: hiring manager, PM partner, engineering lead, values round]

1. Build a table: my stories as rows, these question types as columns: Why here, Impact, Conflict, Data nobody wanted, Failure, What-do-you-do-when, Ambiguity, AI in your work, Leading people (only if I'm a manager). Mark X for a strong fit and ~ for a fit with reframing.
2. Flag any column with fewer than two stories. Those are the gaps to fill before the loop, and tell me which kickoff session fills each one (1 work and scope, 2 best projects, 3 working with people, 4 mistakes and growth, 5 leading a team).
3. For each round in my loop, pick the 2 or 3 question types I'm most likely to get, and assign a lead story and a backup, so no story is told twice in the loop.
4. Use only my stories. No em dashes.
```
