## Patterns
1. **Process before point of view.** Both answers list steps instead of opening with a hypothesis.
2. **No numbers.** You have a 12% story and a 4-day-vs-2-week story in your database and used neither number.
3. **Underselling.** "I just helped" and "we decided" hide that you did the analysis and made the call.
4. **Folding under pushback.** When told "it's seasonal," you agreed instead of saying what data would prove or disprove it.
