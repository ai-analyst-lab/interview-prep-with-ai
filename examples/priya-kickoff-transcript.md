# Example: kickoff transcript (session 2, best projects)

This is a made-up example. Priya is a fictional senior data analyst at Fernly, a fictional meal-kit subscription app. It shows what a casual, unstructured kickoff recording sounds like. Notice it rambles, undersells, and skips numbers. That's fine. The extraction prompt fixes it.

---

**Priya:** Okay, best projects. Um, so the one I always think of first is the orders drop thing from last spring. We came in on a Monday and weekly orders were down, I want to say around 12 percent, and everyone kind of panicked because it was right before the quarterly review. My manager asked me to look into it. So I just started slicing it, by platform, by new versus existing customers, by region. And it was basically all iOS. Android was flat, web was flat. So then I lined it up against the release calendar and there was an iOS release on the Thursday before. I pulled the funnel and the drop was all at the payment step, like people were getting to checkout and not finishing. So it wasn't really a demand problem, it was a bug. The new release broke Apple Pay for some users. I sent it to the iOS team with the funnel chart, they confirmed it, and they shipped a hotfix within, I think, two days. And then orders came back. I think leadership was happy because it meant we didn't have to explain a real drop in the review. And after that I set up an alert on checkout completion by platform so we'd catch it faster next time. I don't know, it was kind of a small thing but people still bring it up.

**Priya:** The other one is the annual plan pricing test. So the growth PM wanted to try a discount on the annual plan, to get more people off monthly. I helped with the experiment. I did the power calculation, figured out we needed about three weeks, set up the metrics with the PM, and then I did the readout. The annual plan uptake went from around 9 percent to 14 percent of new subscribers. So that was good. But the thing I pushed on was that we also had to look at revenue, because a discount can win on uptake and lose on money. I think it was positive on revenue overall, I don't remember the exact number. We rolled it out to everyone after that.

**Priya:** Oh, and there was a time the PM wanted to call a test early because it looked positive after four days, and I said no, we need the full two weeks because weekends behave totally differently for us. People order on Sundays. That was a bit awkward. But we waited, and by the end it was flat, so we didn't ship it. I think that saved us from shipping something that did nothing.

**Priya:** And then, I mean, I also built the churn dashboard that the whole retention team uses, but that's more just a dashboard. It has cohort retention curves by acquisition channel, and I think it gets looked at every week in the retention meeting. That's about it for projects, I think.
