# Example: transcript of a practice mock interview

This is a made-up example of a recorded **practice mock** Priya did with a friend playing the interviewer. Use it to try `/debrief` (Claude Code) or the Debrief mode (any chat app). Priya and Fernly are fictional.

---

**Interviewer:** Thanks for joining. Let's start with a case. Say you're at a subscription meal-kit company and weekly active subscribers dropped 6% last week. How would you approach it?

**Priya:** Sure. So, um, I think the first thing I'd want to do is understand the data. I'd look at where the data comes from, make sure the pipeline is working, and then I'd probably look at different segments, like platform, region, new versus existing. And then I'd look at seasonality, because there could be holidays or something. And then once I have all of that, I'd probably form some hypotheses and test them.

**Interviewer:** Okay. What would you actually expect to find?

**Priya:** It depends. It could be a lot of things. It could be a tracking issue, it could be a product change, it could be a competitor, or marketing spend going down. I'd want to look at all of them.

**Interviewer:** Suppose the drop is only on iOS. What now?

**Priya:** Oh, then I'd check the release calendar. We actually had this happen, I just helped figure out that a release broke something in checkout. So I'd look at the funnel and see which step dropped.

**Interviewer:** Got it. Last one on this. Say the product team says the drop is fine because it's seasonal. How would you respond?

**Priya:** I'd probably say we should check last year's data, and then, yeah, I guess if it's seasonal then it's fine. But we'd want to be sure.

**Interviewer:** Let's switch. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a stakeholder.

**Priya:** So we had a PM who wanted to end a test early because it looked positive. We talked about it and we decided to wait the full two weeks, because weekends are different for us. And it ended up flat. So I think it worked out.

**Interviewer:** What did you say to the PM, exactly?

**Priya:** I think I just said, you know, we should be careful, it's early. And they agreed eventually.
